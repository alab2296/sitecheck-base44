# Prompts used

## Prompt 1: build the app
Build a mobile-friendly web app called "SiteCheck" for small inspection businesses. Users log in with email. There are two roles: Inspector and Admin. The first user to sign up becomes the Admin.

Data: Sites (name, address, client name), Inspections (site, inspector, date, status draft or completed, overall notes), Checklist items (inspection, title, result pass, fail or needs work, notes, photos), Issues (inspection, description, priority low, medium or high, due date, status open or fixed).

Screens: 1) Dashboard with counts of inspections, open issues and overdue issues, and a list of recent inspections. 2) Sites list and add/edit site. 3) New inspection: choose a site and the checklist is created automatically with these items: Walls and paint; Ceiling (stains, cracks); Floors; Windows (open, close, lock); Doors and door locks; Smoke alarm; Kitchen appliances (oven, hob, fridge); Kitchen sink and taps; Bathroom (toilet, shower, drainage); Water pressure and hot water; Electrical sockets and light switches; Heating and ventilation. For each item the inspector sets a result, adds notes and uploads photos from the phone camera. 4) Inspection detail with an "Add issue" button. 5) Report page: a clean printable layout with the site details, a summary of pass, fail and needs-work counts, each checklist item with its photos, and the issue list, plus a "Print / Save as PDF" button.

Rules: Inspectors see only their own inspections. Admins see everything and can manage users. Keep the design simple, with large buttons for use on a phone.

## Prompt 2: print-ready report
Fix the report page so it prints cleanly as a client report. 1) When printing or saving as PDF, hide the app header (including the user's email) and the bottom navigation bar completely, and make sure no checklist item is hidden or cut off between pages. 2) Show checklist photos larger, about 40% of the page width each, and keep each item together with its photos on the same page. 3) Add a "Not inspected" status instead of "Pending" in the report, and show those items in a short grey list at the end instead of mixed in with the inspected items. 4) Show the status Completed once the inspection is marked complete. 5) Add a footer with "Report generated by SiteCheck" and the page number. Don't change anything else in the app.

## Prompt 3: branding and finishing features
Add company branding and a few finishing features. 1) Add a Settings page, visible to Admins only, where they enter a company name, a contact email, a phone number and upload a logo. 2) Show the logo, company name and contact details at the top of the printed report. If no logo is set, show only the company name. 3) Show the inspection's "Overall notes" on the report under the summary. 4) On the inspections list, add a search box (by site name) and keep the All / Draft / Completed filter. 5) Let Admins delete an inspection after a confirmation message, and let inspectors delete only their own drafts. 6) On a completed inspection, show a clear "Completed" label and don't allow editing checklist results unless an Admin reopens it. Don't change anything else.
